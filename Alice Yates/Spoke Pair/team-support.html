Support for Spoke Pair

Spoke Pair is a free iOS app by Alice Yates. Email alice.yates30@yahoo.com.

Tap a spoke to add one turn. The rim pulls toward that spoke. The opposite spoke has to take the same number of turns before the pair is even. Close the pass only when both pairs match and every spoke has left zero. Abandon throws away only the turns in this pass.

Wheels stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not turn a real wheel. Questions: alice.yates30@yahoo.com.
