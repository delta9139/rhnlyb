Privacy Policy for Spoke Pair

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Spoke Pair is a free iOS app made by Alice Yates, an individual developer. Contact: alice.yates30@yahoo.com. Postal contact: United States.

The app stores the wheels, spoke names, the turns you add, and the notes from closing or abandoning a pass on this iPhone. A short summary can be shared only when you tap Share.
