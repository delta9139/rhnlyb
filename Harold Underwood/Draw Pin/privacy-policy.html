Privacy Policy for Draw Pin

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Draw Pin is a free iOS app made by Harold Underwood, an individual developer. Contact: harold.underwood95@163.com. Postal contact: United States.

The app stores the joints, the shoulder, the pin offset, whether the pin is in, and the notes from driving or pulling the pin on this iPhone. A short summary can be shared only when you tap Share. All of this stays on the phone unless you choose to share that summary. The app does not require an account, does not sell anything, and does not track you.

Draw Pin does not collect personal data, does not use advertising, and does not use third-party analytics.

The app is intended for a general audience. The age rating is 0.
