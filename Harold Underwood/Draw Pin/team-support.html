Support for Draw Pin

Draw Pin is a free iOS app by Harold Underwood. Email harold.underwood95@163.com.

Set the pin hole toward the shoulder and drive the pin to pull that shoulder home. A hole offset away from the shoulder pushes the joint open and the pin pops back. A hole with no offset only passes through. A hole larger than the tenon misses. Pull the pin out when it is already in. The shoulder stays where it is.

Joints stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not drive a real pin. Questions: harold.underwood95@163.com.
