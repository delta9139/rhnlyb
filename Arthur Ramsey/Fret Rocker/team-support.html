Fret Rocker is a free iOS app by Arthur Ramsey. Email arthur.ramsey11@outlook.com.

Seat the rocker on three frets. File only the fret it names. A stroke that would fall below both neighbors stays put. The neck is dressed when every window sits.

Necks stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not dress a real guitar. Questions: arthur.ramsey11@outlook.com.
