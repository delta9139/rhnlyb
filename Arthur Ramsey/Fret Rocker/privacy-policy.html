Privacy Policy for Fret Rocker

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Fret Rocker is a free iOS app made by Arthur Ramsey, an individual developer. Contact: arthur.ramsey11@outlook.com. Postal contact: United States.

The app stores each neck, its name, the fret heights, and the rocker window on this iPhone. A short summary can be shared only when you tap Share. All of this stays on the phone unless you choose to share that summary. The app does not require an account, does not sell anything, and does not track you.

Fret Rocker does not collect personal data, does not use advertising, and does not use third-party analytics.

The app is intended for a general audience. The age rating is 0.
