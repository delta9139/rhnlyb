Support for Mortise Chop

Mortise Chop is a free iOS app by Carl Ward. Email carl.ward17@yahoo.com.

A chip leaves only when the chisel is between the two knife lines and the bevel faces the waste. On a knife line the chip springs back. Facing the cheek crushes the cheek and the chip springs back. A chip already at the knife depth stays there.

Mortises stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not chop the wood for you. Questions: carl.ward17@yahoo.com.
