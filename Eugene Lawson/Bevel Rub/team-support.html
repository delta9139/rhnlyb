Support for Bevel Rub

Bevel Rub is a free iOS app by Eugene Lawson. Email eugene.lawson41@gmail.com.

Keep the bevel on the wood, then take a shallow pass. If the edge leads, the tool digs in and the diameter stays. A pass deeper than 0.25, or a cut that would leave less than 8 mm, also leaves the diameter.

Spindles stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not turn wood on a real lathe. Questions: eugene.lawson41@gmail.com.
