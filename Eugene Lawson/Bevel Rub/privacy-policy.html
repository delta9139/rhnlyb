Privacy Policy for Bevel Rub

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Bevel Rub is a free iOS app made by Eugene Lawson, an individual developer. Contact: eugene.lawson41@gmail.com. Postal contact: United States.

The app stores each spindle, its diameter, the bevel angle, the depth of the pass, and the mark from taking that pass on this iPhone. A four-line summary can be shared only when you tap Share.
