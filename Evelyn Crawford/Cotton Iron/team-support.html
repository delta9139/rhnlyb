Support for Cotton Iron

Cotton Iron is a free iOS app by Evelyn Crawford. Email evelyn.crawford70@126.com.

Drive the iron into the seam. Cotton stays only in the middle. On the plank it bruises the wood. Too shallow and the seam weeps. Driven through, the cotton drops. Pitch covers only a seam that already holds.

Seams stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not caulk a real boat. Questions: evelyn.crawford70@126.com.
