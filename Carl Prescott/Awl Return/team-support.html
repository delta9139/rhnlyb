Support for Awl Return

Awl Return is a free iOS app by Carl Prescott. Email carl.prescott68@outlook.com.

A stitch locks only when the awl sits on the mark and the thread comes back through that same hole. In the flesh, or off the edge, the awl springs back. Through the next hole the thread crosses and is pulled back. A stitch that has already passed stays put.

Seams stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not stitch the leather for you. Questions: carl.prescott68@outlook.com.
