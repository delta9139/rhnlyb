Privacy Policy for Came Butt

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Came Butt is a free iOS app made by Arthur Brooks, an individual developer. Contact: arthur.brooks83@126.com. Postal contact: United States.

The app stores the panels, cames, solder joints, glass names, and sizes you enter on this iPhone. A photo you choose is kept only as a picture under that panel. A panel summary can be shared only when you tap Share. All of this stays on the phone unless you choose to share that summary. The app does not require an account, does not sell anything, and does not track you.

Came Butt does not collect personal data, does not use advertising, and does not use third-party analytics.

The app is intended for a general audience. The age rating is 0.
