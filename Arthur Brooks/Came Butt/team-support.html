Support for Came Butt

Came Butt is a free iOS app by Arthur Brooks. Email arthur.brooks83@126.com.

A came stays when both ends meet another came or the border. An end that lands in the glass springs back. Solder stays on a joint. A glass piece keeps a name only when every joint around it is soldered. Changing the panel size can spring a came whose ends no longer meet.

Panels stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: arthur.brooks83@126.com.
