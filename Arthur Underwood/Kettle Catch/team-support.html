Support for Kettle Catch

Kettle Catch is a free iOS app by Arthur Underwood. Email arthur.underwood44@sina.com.

The needle enters the head of the signature, catches the previous kettle once, and leaves through the tail. Coming back out pulls the thread. Catching twice springs back. The book is sewn when every signature is locked.

Books stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not sew a paper book. Questions: arthur.underwood44@sina.com.
