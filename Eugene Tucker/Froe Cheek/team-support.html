Support for Froe Cheek

Froe Cheek is a free iOS app by Eugene Tucker. Email eugene.tucker56@163.com.

Drag the froe across the end grain, then drive it along the ray. The middle third rives. An outer quarter runs out the side. If that run-out is still in the last quarter, back the froe and twist the other way. If it left earlier, the twist is refused.

Billets stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not split real wood. Questions: eugene.tucker56@163.com.
