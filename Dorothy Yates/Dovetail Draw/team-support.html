Support for Dovetail Draw

Dovetail Draw is a free iOS app by Dorothy Yates. Email dorothy.yates51@yahoo.com.

A pin draws when both cheeks narrow toward the neck and the end does not stand past the socket. A cheek that flares pushes the tail out. A square cheek leaves a gap. Pare the end only when it stands proud. A drawn pin keeps its cheeks.

Drawers stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not cut a wooden drawer. Questions: dorothy.yates51@yahoo.com.
