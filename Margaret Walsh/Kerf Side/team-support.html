Support for Kerf Side

Kerf Side is a free iOS app by Margaret Walsh. Email margaret.walsh10@sina.com.

Draw the kerf on the waste side of the chalk line. A sample that crosses into the face stays into the face. A drift wider than the line must come back inside the window, or the kerf is left wide. A stroke that stops short of the far end is short.

Boards stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not saw a real board. Questions: margaret.walsh10@sina.com.
