Support for Cone Pack

Cone Pack is a free iOS app by Frank Ramsey. Email frank.ramsey21@163.com.

Three witness cones stand from cooler to hotter. A hotter cone cannot bend farther than the cooler cone beside it. Draw the kiln only when the firing cone is down and the guard cone is still up. If the guard cone is down, the firing is overfired.

Firings stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: frank.ramsey21@163.com.
