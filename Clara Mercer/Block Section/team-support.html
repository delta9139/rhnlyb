Support for Block Section

Block Section is a free iOS app by Clara Mercer. Email clara.mercer35@outlook.com.

Drag the staff onto the train, then drag the train into the next section. Without the staff, the train stays and the try is refused. Releasing the train in the same section does not count.

Lines stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not control a real railway. Questions: clara.mercer35@outlook.com.
