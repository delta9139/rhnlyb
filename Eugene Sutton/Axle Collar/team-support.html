Support for Axle Collar

Axle Collar is a free iOS app by Eugene Sutton. Email eugene.sutton69@gmail.com.

Face the collar out and drop the peg into the next empty hole. A collar that faces in pops back, and so does a peg dropped into the wrong hole. The cart rolls when both pegs stay seated.

Carts stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not turn a real axle. Questions: eugene.sutton69@gmail.com.
