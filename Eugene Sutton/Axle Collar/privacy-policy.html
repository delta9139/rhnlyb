Privacy Policy for Axle Collar

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Axle Collar is a free iOS app made by Eugene Sutton, an individual developer. Contact: eugene.sutton69@gmail.com. Postal contact: United States.

The app stores the carts, hole names, which pegs are seated, and the pop notes you enter on this iPhone. A short summary can be shared only when you tap Share.
