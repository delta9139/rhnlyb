Support for Stick Sight

Stick Sight is a free iOS app by Frank Harmon. Email frank.harmon24@126.com.

Two winding sticks sit across the ends of a board. Plane only a corner on the high diagonal. The other diagonal is refused, and the thicknesses stay. When the sticks are already parallel, even a thicker side is left alone.

Boards stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not plane the wood for you. Questions: frank.harmon24@126.com.
