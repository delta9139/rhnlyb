Support for Champion Tooth

Champion Tooth is a free iOS app by Arthur Lawson. Email arthur.lawson42@sina.com.

A group is a left cutter, a right cutter, and a raker. A cutter stays only on its own side. Bend it the other way and it springs back. A raker springs straight. The saw clears when every raker is straight and every cutter is on its own side.

Saws stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. The app does not bend a real saw. Questions: arthur.lawson42@sina.com.
